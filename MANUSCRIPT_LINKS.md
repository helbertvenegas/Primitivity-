# Traceability to the manuscript

The full ledger is `reports/coverage.json` and its readable version is
`reports/coverage.md`. It inventories all 108 labels in the supplied snapshot.
The verifier fails if any required computational block has no labeled check.

Each computational record has `manuscript_labels`, an exact residual or
certificate, and a pass/fail flag. The ledger distinguishes checked algebraic
ingredients, mathematical/theoretical dependencies and open questions.

The actual closed degree is computed independently from the torus and the
center-action image in all 21 implemented models. `pi_decision` remains an
instantiation of the PI criterion table, not an independent theorem proof.
